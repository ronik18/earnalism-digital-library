# Viewer-feedback coverage

This maps the simulated viewer's qualities to generic production work. **A** means executed artifact/schema/technical evidence; **H** means an actual human judgment or uncoached participant response; **M** means real post-publication metrics with format, traffic source, date, denominator and limitations. A plan, simulated critique or automated pass cannot supply H or M. Use the supported plan schema; do not invent APIs or mark unfinished artifacts complete.

| Feedback quality | Concrete action and generation artifact/check | Evidence needed to assess the result |
|---|---|---|
| A relevant reason to stop | Record intended viewer, prior knowledge, consequential question and one complete learning promise. Distinguish familiar facts from the new insight. | A: completed audience promise. H: unprompted relevance/novelty explanation; audience fit is not assumed from topic alone. |
| A compelling, truthful reason to click | Choose one truthful title per format; alternatives are optional; make minimal thumbnail copy complement the title and one focal image. | A: real selected cover/title assets and bindings. H: `packaging_truth_and_readability` plus uncoached card choice. M: comparable source-specific discovery results; no declared winner from intuition. |
| Packaging readable at browse size | Inspect actual 320×180 and 160×90 landscape readbacks and the intended portrait cover/UI. Remove competing copy rather than shrinking it indefinitely. | A: saved small previews. H: actual readable words, focal hierarchy and truthful promise at those sizes. |
| A meaningful first frame | Plan a relevant object or evidence immediately visible; avoid a decorative blank or added logo delay. | A: storyboard and actual first-frame reference in both exports. H: reviewer identifies the subject/question without coaching. |
| A worthwhile early payoff | Plan the first consequential visual answer and its time for this audience and narration; do not impose universal seconds. | A: timed opening/payoff map and final-media evidence. H: `opening_visual_payoff`. M: retention around that actual event, interpreted with context. |
| A reason to continue through every scene | Map **viewer question → explanatory visual answer → bridge** for every scene, with source/model and timing bindings. Remove scenes without a learning purpose. | A: complete scene map and accurate topic tests. H: actual motion/joins answer the questions coherently; M: scene-specific confusion/retention questions, not automatic causal claims. |
| Animation that explains instead of decorating | Choose topic-relevant mechanisms, comparisons, charts or evidence; keep state and referents consistent. | A: researched model, scene/transition tests and actual renders. H: strict graphical/factual review. Non-lunar topics require new tested renderer capability; relabeling Moon scenes fails. |
| Understanding without overload | Stage essential labels, remove redundant annotations, and allow real reading/inspection time without stretching accepted speech. | A: captions/anchors/layout and actual transition frames. H: narrow/fullscreen reading and one-view explanation; desktop fixtures do not replace a physical-device check. |
| Active prediction, recall or application | Where useful, design a consequential prompt, adequate pause/hold and accurate reveal; preserve captions and the neutral study protocol. | A: prompt/reveal map with actual timing. H: independent response and misconception evidence. One correct recall does not establish lifelong retention. |
| A complete, honest payoff | Match the title and opening promise to the explanation and conclusion; state necessary qualifications and scope. | A: promise-to-source/scene coverage. H: reviewer can explain the promised relationship; all unresolved factual claims block release. |
| An engaging trusted voice | Preserve current B/audio for visual/packaging work; keep approved original direction for authorized generation and assess specific defects before changing it. | A: protected audio hashes and actual-audio timing. H: real full listening and paragraph continuity/naturalness review. WPM, age prompts and celebrity resemblance are not acceptance. |
| Music that helps attention and clarity | Retain accepted score/silences unless a specific audible defect justifies an authorized correction; avoid gratuitous timing or loudness revisions. | A: source/balance/transition/silence checks. H: scored/dry, headphones/speakers and resemblance review. No therapeutic or attention guarantee. |
| Accessible format-specific viewing | Give landscape and independently composed Shorts explicit purposes and complete payoffs; retain caption access, hierarchy and safe areas. | A: actual assets/layout/caption checks. H: both full playthroughs and device reading. M: evaluate formats and their traffic sources separately. |
| Friction-free watching and replay | Exercise visible motion, caption gaps, pause/seek/reopen, fullscreen and Replay; verify actual retrieved files. | A: executed player/hash/decode evidence. H: real device acceptance. A moving time counter alone is insufficient. |
| A reason to subscribe or return | State a repeatable learning-series promise and an honest available/planned/absent next lesson; preserve the standard ending. | A: next-step state and available-link readback. H: viewer can describe the expected future value. M: actual return/next-lesson/subscriber evidence; no fabricated catalogue or guaranteed loyalty. |
| Consistency that is sustainable | Optionally plan cadence from actual production/review capacity and retain a per-episode time/compute/revision journal and preserve failed attempts. | A: measured cost/capacity and cadence plan. M: actual delivery/quality over time. Planning is not scheduling, paid allowance, bulk production or permission to skip gates. |
| Future topics led by viewers | Turn real questions, confusions and relevant requests into a ranked candidate backlog with fit, risk, model capability and effort. | A: source-linked rationale and feasibility. H: real requests/feedback with sample limits. No guessed trends, global top-three data or invented people. |
| Honest distribution and improvement | Complete current official-policy/manual Studio processing, captions, AI/audience/copyright and Shorts checks; observe configurable 48 h/7 d/28 d checkpoints after actual publication. | A/H: actual upload/processing records and final approvals. M: real comparable Analytics observations; no fixed YPP threshold, algorithm deadline or automatic-upload authority. |
| Commercial value grounded in evidence | Use existing authorized next-step/library paths and record actual attributable outcomes and known production costs. | A: implemented destination and scoped journal. M: observed conversions/revenue if available, with limits. No new CMS/payments, promised earnings or claim that views alone create revenue. |
| Protected inputs and isolated work | Before/after original-notebook checksum; separate video notebook, config/log/cache/output roots and immutable parent editions. | A: verified source hashes and isolated paths; no production overwrite. |
| Real narration with bounded resources | Pinned same-provider selected B, persistent validation/retries, free-first compute, scoped metered allowance and durable verified transfer/shutdown. | A: actual audio/provenance/import evidence; silent fixtures are not integration. H: new actual speech listening. |
| Accurate readable captions | Current-script-bound ASR reconciliation and actual-word phrase timing, caption gates, separate supplementary explanation and delivered caption access. | A: hashes, resolved differences, SRT/VTT and final burned-caption frames. H: reading and listening, not ASR similarity alone. |
| Unobstructed labels throughout motion | Paths first, then protected foreground glyphs; trajectory/mask tests, named regression checkpoints and final encoded label evidence in both formats. | A: actual samples and regressions. H: semantic/graphical judgment; pixel counts are not OCR or approval. |
| Inherited original branding | Exact full website logo-and-text lockup, approved three-second page-turn END ident, no spoken brand or repeated body strip; accessible sources/disclosure. | A: asset/voice/default drift checks and final ending frames; rights attestations reused only while valid. |
| Efficient truthful verification | Quick checks by default; full mode discovers every supported test directory; one consolidated final candidate decision. | A: executed checks and actual file delivery; H: primary factual/AV decision, never inferred from tests. |

The standards and planner are topic-agnostic; choose an adapter only when its actual model and tested capabilities match the topic. Bread-making mechanisms, electric circuits and historical-map comparisons illustrate distinct designs in [viewer experience](references/VIEWER_EXPERIENCE.md), not existing supported renders or verified episodes. Current B and all current Moon media remain unchanged. Never substitute automatic coverage, a simulated full-watch/subscription judgment or a self-score for strict human factual, graphical, listening and artifact-bound publication decisions.
