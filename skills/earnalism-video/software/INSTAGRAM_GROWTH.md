# Instagram: validate advice, remove friction, test the learning promise

Use when evaluating creator growth claims or preparing Instagram profile/series improvements. This supplements PLATFORM_GROWTH.md; it does not add release gates or authority to change account settings. Consult actual UI because control names and availability differ. No setting guarantees virality, exponential followers or revenue.

Use the [Reels playbook](GROWTH_PLAYBOOKS.md#instagram-reels) for concrete creation/packaging decisions and [growth feedback](GROWTH_FEEDBACK.md) for native denominators, matched-age experiments and adopt/revise/retire rules. These are reusable defaults and optional learning tools, not additional release approvals.

## Separate mechanism, anecdote and causal evidence

A creator's before/after story or public follower count does not isolate a setting's effect. Record competing changes (creative, topic, timing, paid distribution, collaborations), missing failed attempts, measurement windows and unknowns. Don't infer “90% viral” repeatability from a selected success sequence. Label a sales-guide funnel or comment-keyword lead magnet as such without assuming dishonesty. Public likes/comments cannot verify follower attribution or profit. If only a Reel's visible caption is inspected, don't imply its spoken track or underlying Insights were reviewed.

First verify relevant account identity, public/professional state and actual recommendation status. Account suggestions are a profile-discovery control, not a universal reach switch. Story reshares, native reposts, third-party embeds, search indexing, downloads, remixing and audio reuse are distinct permissions. Never turn them all on under “growth optimisation”; respect existing rights/reuse preferences and owner authority. Already-enabled settings don't explain a missing viral unlock. Eligible content can still receive little distribution.

External indexing helps possible search discovery, not guaranteed Reel reach; embedding permits display on third-party sites, not compulsory algorithm eligibility. Check current official scope and the owner’s preference before changing either. Do not enable product featuring, automatic commercial-request approval, account permissions, unreviewed dubs or enhancements as collateral changes.

## Make the profile and the Reel agree

A useful profile answers: who is this for, what useful experience is delivered, and why return? Propose concise concrete copy while retaining Earnalism's digital-library, reading, selected-audiobook and visual-learning identity. A bio experiment isn't an on-screen tagline or a rebrand. Where useful, propose three existing approved public pins: start-here demonstration, strongest representative complete lesson, and connected follow-up. Verify availability/crops; don't expose private drafts or promise a schedule that isn't confirmed. Profile/pin proposals require no mandatory overhaul before episode release.

Use a truthful hook that previews the actual demonstration. Prefer a familiar contradiction, prediction or consequential question. Identity/age/credential/numerical formats are optional writing devices; never invent personal experience, authority, scientific claims or results. Avoid addressing a narrow personal-brand demographic unless the actual audience calls for it. Deliver an answer before opening a meaningful next question. Rehooks should explain a new connection rather than postpone the promised answer, overload the picture or enforce an arbitrary quota. Preserve accepted B unless an audio change is separately authorized.

Write natural topic terms in relevant profile/caption copy and suitable on-screen labels; keep accurate accessibility text. Never paste keyword blocks, use invisible text or claim that asking Meta AI for keywords controls distribution. Keep the owner’s exact pair #earnalism #learnalism, plus relevant topic tags. No invented fixed keyword/hashtag count or ranking multiplier.

Use one primary relevant invitation, with an actual next lesson/library destination where available. Give genuine save/share value through the explanation itself. Don't force unrelated comments, copied scripts or “comment a word” automations just to inflate metrics. If a legitimate lead magnet is explicitly requested later, verify it exists, can be delivered, and any messaging/permission/consent requirements; don't send DMs or set up a new funnel from a strategy request.

Make the recurring viewpoint concrete: what does Earnalism help this audience understand, and what recognizable explanatory approach makes returning useful? Where an offer needs supporting proof, prefer an inspectable lesson or product sample. Use permissioned reader/listener feedback or documented outcomes only when relevant and authentic; preserve context and attribution. Never invent clients, testimonials, credentials or results, or imply a story establishes typical outcomes. Engineering checks and self-scores are not customer-benefit evidence. Such proof is optional when no commercial claim requires it; it creates no extra release approval or requirement to collect testimonials.

Treat “stop → follow → choose” as a qualitative editorial lens, not a guaranteed linear buying journey. Unsourced infographic percentages such as 40/30/20/10 are not verified psychological findings, Instagram ranking weights or content-time quotas. Judge viewing choice, repeat/follow behaviour and actual attributable commercial outcomes separately.

## Measure the proposed mechanism

Mosseri's verified original caption recommends average Reel watch time, likes per person reached and sends per person reached [1]. These are useful diagnostics, not a disclosed fixed algorithm or a guarantee. Calculate only with the exact native denominators: likes/views isn't likes/reach; shares isn't necessarily private sends; displayed viewers isn't automatically accounts reached. Keep unavailable rates null. Compare viewing, follow attribution, return behaviour and attributable business outcomes separately. A smaller Reel with a higher like ratio need not produce more total satisfied viewers, followers or revenue.

Test one coherent hypothesis at comparable publication ages, durations and audience/traffic mix. Retain source, sample counts, changed variables and processing limits. One creator interview/newsletter can inspire an experiment; it doesn't transfer that creator's results to Earnalism. Inconclusive is valid. Don't require arbitrary sample thresholds or optional experiments for GO LIVE, and don't loop public uploads for QA.

Revenue options depend on actual account/country eligibility and real product fit. Inspect available monetization features before forecasting. Do not equate discovery with sales or manufacture RPM/conversion/earnings. Preserve existing measurement, costs and authorization boundaries. Choose a sustainable cadence from capacity and evidence rather than a universal posting-time or daily quota. Private drafts remain private until the existing consolidated release decision.
