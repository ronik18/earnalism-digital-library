# Video delivery and audio masters

## Default playback delivery

Deliver MP4 with progressive H.264 video, yuv420p chroma, BT.709 SDR colour and AAC-LC stereo at 48 kHz. Preserve the authored frame rate (normally 30 fps) and the separately composed 1920×1080 landscape / 1080×1920 portrait layouts. Put the MP4 moov metadata before media data (+faststart), verify seek/reload/play-to-end and check actual encoded streams and dimensions. Choose quality-based encoding and inspect detailed motion; a file extension alone does not determine quality, compatibility or performance. Use a smaller derivative when actual delivery constraints justify it, retaining a high-quality source master. Do not silently upscale or interpolate source media and call it native higher-resolution footage. Record actual returned clip dimensions even when the provider ignored the requested resolution.

When a caption overlay ends before the film, pass through the underlying picture instead of shortening it or repeating the last caption into the ending. Verify the actual final frame count and complete approved ident; keep a focused regression for this failure.

Keep a clean master with the actual reviewed caption track and a destination-specific burned-caption copy where needed; avoid stacked native and burned captions. Derive all caption timing from the actual unchanged audio. A WAV is not the video soundtrack container for ordinary web playback; the MP4 contains its own AAC audio.

## Audio preservation

Retain PCM WAV narration and the complete mixed soundtrack as audio masters, normally at the existing 48 kHz project rate. A 24-bit PCM export is useful for editing headroom; it cannot add detail missing from a compressed source or earlier lower-bit-depth recording. Preserve original provider files and their hashes. Prefer the existing verified AAC payload for visual-only revisions instead of unnecessary audio re-encoding; verify identical packet payloads across delivered video formats. Report the source and derivation of each WAV honestly. Do not confuse .wav, .mp4 or a successful decode with listening acceptance.

Reset decoded timestamp origin when assembling segments; check actual sample durations, narration plus intentional rests and the full approved ending sound. Do not loosen duration checks to hide a clipped tail. Retain earlier failed exports and invalidate only affected evidence. Technical validation does not supply factual, rights, naturalness or pronunciation approval.

## Reference checked 2026-11-07

YouTube's official [recommended upload encoding settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1722171?hl=en) supports MP4 fast start, H.264 progressive 4:2:0, 48 kHz AAC and BT.709 SDR. This is platform guidance, not a universal best-codec claim or a requirement to replace an already qualified unchanged soundtrack. Recheck destination requirements at actual delivery.

## Consumer-player verification

Check native captions against the complete logo lockup as well as headings, diagrams and controls; a clean master alone does not prevent a two-line caption from reaching into the watermark. Correct only the delivery sidecar when sufficient, preserving every word and timestamp, and verify the saved selected track in the actual destination player. A stale “captions unavailable” accessibility label can contradict visible English captions and the selected English menu item; record the contradiction and test the rendered track instead of diagnosing missing captions from the label alone.

For looping Reels/Shorts, record the exact post ID, duration and complete played range or observed repeat; `ended=false` alone is not a playback failure. Distinguish paused seeks from continuous coverage, source resolution from the adaptive served rendition, and desktop evidence from native-mobile or signed-out verification. Retain the scope of the checks actually performed.

A local caption rail outside the video is excluded by video-only fullscreen. Use one real caption track in a clean preview, or a fullscreen container that also includes the rail; avoid duplicating native, external and burned captions. Check a long cue while paused and after seeking, caption toggling and fullscreen where the available controls permit it. Record a failed or unavailable fullscreen test explicitly rather than claiming it passed or changing accepted audio.

## Instagram frame-corruption prevention and triage (2026-11-09)

Before an Instagram upload, inspect the exact destination file with `python3 software/instagram_preflight.py INPUT.mp4 --report REPORT.json` (project: `video_studio/instagram_preflight.py`). Reuse a valid receipt for unchanged file bytes. The helper checks fast-start structure, edit lists, progressive H.264 4:2:0/AAC metadata and strict full decoding. Its technical result does **not** certify visual or native-mobile playback. Retain original approved masters and their accepted soundtrack.

Meta's [official publishing sample specification](https://github.com/fbsamples/reels_publishing_apis/blob/main/insta_reels_publishing_api_sample/README.md#reels-requirements-for-publishing) specifies no MP4 edit lists, front-loaded moov, progressive closed-GOP H.264/HEVC and 4:2:0. Use these as conservative Instagram delivery targets; browser/app behavior is still separately verified. Do not assume generic MP4 qualification covers every destination. Keep project 30fps/SDR/square-pixel output and independent portrait composition; avoid unsolicited codec/bitrate changes to qualified YouTube media.

If edit lists or timestamp offsets exist, prepare a **separate private compatibility derivative**, inspect its first frame, full duration, final ident, seek/loop behavior, caption sync and audio/video start offset before promoting it. A `-c copy -use_editlist 0` remux can preserve compressed packet payloads while changing presentation timing; payload equality alone is insufficient. Never replace the approved candidate or upload an experimental derivative automatically.

For reported tearing, repeated/tiled strips, flashes or corrupted logos: preserve the owner screenshot and exact post ID; compare the source opening/motion/transitions/ending against the served rendition. Check preview start, playback, seek and loop reset. A successful decoder return code does not detect all visibly wrong pixels. Use actual visual checks; do not invent an automatic flicker detector from unrelated frame-difference thresholds. If a supported Android/native view is available, inspect it; otherwise state that scope is unverified. Desktop playback cannot clear an owner-reported mobile defect. Known corruption remains an unresolved delivery issue until the affected surface is rechecked; do not create another routine approval gate for unaffected posts.

Keep source encoding errors, platform-transcode issues and device/app-decoder hypotheses distinct. Clean source and one intact desktop rendition do not identify the Android root cause. Do not blame the device, prescribe universal Baseline/720p/no-B-frame output, claim a guaranteed cure, delete/repost, change app/security settings or replace accepted audio without evidence and the relevant authority. Capture a supported problematic rendition when reliably bound to the post; do not guess among unrelated feed assets or bypass blob/download limitations.

### Source-first pixel isolation and repair

The exact-frame source/rendition investigation found tiled pictures already present in the initial silent animation and retained in Instagram's delivered stream. Earlier sparse clean screenshots and successful decoding did not establish source pixel correctness. Treat earlier decoder/resolution hypotheses below as historical diagnostics, not evidence that the source was clean.

For reported tearing, compare the affected frame **and adjacent frames** in the uploaded master, earliest available animation and reliably post-bound delivered representation before attributing the symptom to a device renderer. Dense opening review matters: intact frame zero can sit between damaged frames; also inspect scene patches/transitions and the ending. For the affected generated-portrait route, include this pixel review in the existing preflight rather than adding another approval. A stable logo-region comparison may help locate outliers in a particular frozen scene, but its thresholds are scene-specific and it cannot certify the whole picture.

If the source is damaged, restore pictures from preserved scene assets and timing. A bounded frame/segment repair is suitable when all identified defects are covered and the remainder is checked; otherwise rerender the affected composition. Reencoding or resizing the damaged source alone cannot reconstruct missing picture content. Review every known failing frame and surrounding motion in the new hash-bound candidate, preserve original AAC payload **and timing/decoded PCM**, and recheck video frame count, captions and ending. Keep accepted source masters and existing posts intact.

Qualify the native test environment with the same moving control across opening, loop and editor transitions. A renderer warning/crash prevents calling that configuration error-free. A documented alternative renderer can be tested without changing the media; retain its bounded scope. After native draft save/reopen, verify the intended full selected duration and opening/seek/loop behavior instead of assuming the original SEND import or saved draft retained it. Native unpublished preview evidence does not certify Instagram's later server-processed public rendition or every handset.

Native editor-generated thumbnails may be corrupted even when current video playback is intact. Inspect the current preview, generated timeline/draft thumbnail and selected cover separately. Tiny timeline-strip text can be blurred by downsampling; compare an enlarged thumbnail and the full-size generated cover before classifying it as frame corruption. An intact cover does not clear a separate playback or renderer failure. A separately imported reviewed cover was saved and confirmed in the draft list in the bounded test; this restores that cover, not the internal thumbnail generator or public delivery. Retain any unresolved thumbnail issue explicitly. Do not infer file corruption or a resolution cure from a cached thumbnail alone.

For an authorized isolated Android import, confirm the exact media URI/hash and that MediaStore indexing has completed (duration/dimensions present; no pending import) before interpreting picker visibility or selection behavior. Finalize a task-owned import only through supported owner controls; do not change unrelated media or broaden permissions. Android's [pending-media documentation](https://developer.android.com/training/data-storage/shared/media#toggle-pending-status) explains visibility while insertion is incomplete. Still inspect the actual native selected duration: both tested resolutions could default to 15 seconds, so resolution/metadata alone does not guarantee a complete selection.

PNG capture and fresh JPEG probes both produced intact pictures in the bounded source repair investigation. This does **not** isolate the intermittent old browser/capture/encoder trigger or establish PNG, a software GPU, 720p, no-B-frames or no-edit-list output as a universal cure. Adopt only the repair and checks actually verified; keep replacements private until reviewed and preserve the one consolidated release decision.

### Qualify Android diagnosis before changing production media

A native screenshot test is valid only when the environment itself passes a known-good moving control. Record emulator/system-image/app versions, graphics/decoder configuration, exact imported file identity and fresh capture timestamps. Check the control advances and can loop/reopen without tiling, black output or graphics-buffer errors. If it fails, mark the environment UNQUALIFIED; do not use that run to approve/reject a production encode or prescribe a universal resolution change.

Never reuse an earlier `uiautomator` XML after a failed/idle-timeout dump. Use a unique output path per dump, require confirmed successful completion and parse only that output. For moving-video checks, use fresh validated PNG captures without overwriting prior evidence; screenshots prove only their observed frames, not continuous full-film playback. Check the actual imported file after app recovery instead of assuming the requested SEND intent replaced a restored editor.

Before a renderer/runtime comparison, verify the task-owned previous emulator process actually exited. A console shutdown acknowledgement alone may leave it hung; do not mistake a duplicate-AVD launch failure for a new test run. Stop only identified task-owned processes, preserve original AVD data, and clone locally before trying an older runtime. Record official archive URL/checksum and license authority; do not clear security/quarantine protections or send crash diagnostics as a workaround. Keep renderer and video-encoder changes in separate comparisons.

### Bound device-specific claims to the tested path

A reported handset model is useful provenance, not proof of its current OS/app build or a diagnosed chipset bug. Record unavailable versions as unknown; never substitute launch specifications for the owner's installed versions. Keep model/build details in project evidence rather than reusable defaults. Official [Android graphics guidance](https://developer.android.com/studio/run/emulator-acceleration) explains that host mode uses the computer GPU and incompatible acceleration can display incorrect images. Changing an AVD name, skin, screen size or Android API does not reproduce a manufacturer's GPU/codec/OS stack. Do not claim a phone-specific repair from an emulator pass.

For a reliable fix claim, bind the failing post/surface, exact candidate hash, full selected duration and observed opening/seek/loop/reopen results to the tested device/app path. Distinguish source-file playback, unpublished composer preview and the platform-processed public rendition; none is interchangeable evidence. Preserve accepted audio and published posts. If only emulator access exists and its renderer produces errors, retain the native-delivery HOLD and report the access gap rather than repeatedly trying speculative codec or resolution changes. Clean time-separated samples are useful bounded observations, not proof of every intervening frame.

## Captured split/tile pixels — source regression gate

A successfully decoded H.264 file may already contain tiled, clipped or repeated captured pixels. On 10 November 2026, independent stills and adjacent-frame inspection reproduced this in all three Ice/Paper Towel/Bus source packages, before Instagram. It is a render-output failure, not proof of an Instagram-only fault; the exact intermittent capture/encoding trigger remains unisolated. A compatibility remux cannot repair pixels already encoded incorrectly. Do not call PNG capture, lower concurrency, 720p, a GPU switch or no B-frames a proven universal cure.

For this affected generated-composition route, use `software/pixel_integrity.py` (project `video_studio/pixel_integrity.py`) before ident assembly and on the final destination MP4. Prepare independently rendered and visually inspected clean stills from the same frozen composition, props and frame numbers. The JSON config uses schema `earnalism.pixel-integrity.v1`, `reference_origin: independent_composition_stills`, `dimensions`, and `checks` containing the relative reference path/SHA256, exclusive `[start,end]` frame range, integer `[x,y,width,height]` rectangle, sample size and calibrated `max_mae`. Require independent full-frame comparisons at frame zero and the last body frame, a stable content-bearing anchor covering every body frame, and a full-body full-picture temporal discontinuity scan. The latter surfaces isolated flashes whose neighbours agree, rather than treating an ordinary persistent scene cut as corruption. It uses a low-resolution luminance probe, not every-pixel or all-colour verification; unexpected targets require actual inspection. Use separate references for genuine background/scene changes; do not increase thresholds or compare a damaged file to itself to obtain a pass. Tolerances and ROIs are composition-specific, not universal constants.

Run `python3 software/pixel_integrity.py INPUT.mp4 --config CONFIG.json --body-frames N --report FRESH_REPORT.json`. A failing, missing or stale receipt holds this known-regression qualification; source, config, reference and checker hashes bind it. The project-local three-pilot route uses `pixel-reference-plan.json` to map `portrait` and `landscape` to reference configs. That route's renderer/checker integration is outside this portable source package: copying these standalone helpers does not wire the generic studio or another topic adapter automatically. Integrate checks before ident assembly and create an exact-final-file receipt after mux; require complete body-anchor/boundary/temporal coverage with `--body-frames` or `validate_delivery_coverage`, not merely `analyze()` or receipt verification alone. Until the matching route and final receipt are actually qualified, keep this gate held. Keep receipts outside immutable historical delivery manifests. The agent prepares these engineering inputs, without another owner form.

Reference-region success establishes only the declared pixel regression. It does not inspect every pixel, certify motion/captions/audio, or prove Android/Instagram acceptance. Also inspect dense opening frames and adjacent frames around scene transitions, large isolated differences and the ending. Unexpected changes require visual diagnosis, not automatic frame replacement. Preserve defective originals; regenerate only demonstrated bad frames from the original composition where feasible, preserve the accepted soundtrack and verify frame counts, AAC payload, packet timing and decoded PCM. Review repaired pixels and final playback privately before replacing the candidate. Keep existing posts untouched unless separately authorized. Never promise that future platform or rendering defects are impossible.

### Actual geometry and low-storage capture

Validate visible rendered glyph bounds and the complete caption box against the same destination review profile as the watermark. A declared `occupiedRects` array alone does not establish actual text size, wrapping, scroll overflow, clip clearance or lower account-row clearance. Measure after layout, before capturing; delay the capture until measurement finishes. Metadata-only mounts may have zero size and are not a rendered-layout pass. Require a receipt covering the actual captured frames. In Remotion’s direct `renderFrames` route, resolved `composition.props` supplies the rendered props; a QA-only flag in raw `inputProps` alone may never run. Preserve metadata calculation while explicitly passing the QA flag to the resolved capture props. The three-pilot route measures actual captions/visible SVG text on every body frame and holds on overflow or UI/caption collision. Its portrait layout correction and contact model are topic-adapter details, not universal coordinates or artwork defaults.

When disk space is constrained, use the tested `software/src/stream_frames.mjs` (project `video_studio/src/stream_frames.mjs`) with the pinned renderer’s public `renderFrames` PNG callback. This profile requires a 30 fps composition and an exact frame count; the disk reserve must be a finite safe integer of at least 600 MiB. It sends one ordered frame at a time to FFmpeg with backpressure instead of accumulating a PNG sequence, refuses missing/out-of-order captures or existing outputs, validates the encoded count and retains at least 600 MiB of free space. Run a small bounded probe first for a new runtime/path; inspect its actual buffer/disk/output measurements. Stop rather than weaken the reserve or remove project files. The project three-pilot renderer defaults to this bounded transport; legacy capture profiles remain explicit diagnostic alternatives. Bounded streaming is a verified storage improvement, **not** proof that PNG, serial capture or FFmpeg has eliminated the intermittent source corruption trigger. Keep pixel and native-platform checks separate.

A source-pixel repair made from an older frozen bundle retains that bundle’s branding and layout. Before calling it the current candidate, reconcile the actual export with applicable accepted owner changes; do not infer compliance from newer source files that were never rendered. Preserve older candidates. For physical explanations, verify visible contact at the exact transition before showing the resulting absorption, force or transfer; the towel/spill fix is a specific regression example, not a prescribed visual for every topic.

Label clearance must also consider visible foreground objects, not only the frame/UI and captions. In the verified three-pilot case, a moving hand crossed a comparison label/heading and a clamp crossed the towel heading during a zoom. The corrected route tags those actual objects, measures their transformed visible bounds before capture and holds on label/object intersection. It also validates the actual transition frames. Reflow or reduce competing reading regions rather than lowering opacity/thresholds to force a pass; do not make every drawing an indiscriminate text-exclusion rectangle or impose these topic-specific arrangements universally.
