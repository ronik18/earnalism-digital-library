# Premium social companions for video releases

Prepare a coordinated promotional companion whenever delivering an Earnalism video to an authorized social channel. Aim to make the lesson easier to discover, understand and revisit; increased viewers is an objective, not a promised result. Reuse the accepted video, research and branding. This reference adds no video-release gate or separate routine approval loop.

## Build the smallest useful set

- Prepare a portrait Story teaser linking to the actual published lesson. Use one strong question, a clear topic illustration or approved video frame, and one invitation to watch. Add a second Story only when it contributes a different useful reason to watch. Never invent a destination before publication; bind the link after the real post URL exists.
- Prepare a short, finished carousel when the explanation benefits from a swipeable recap: question → mechanism → takeaway → related lesson or library invitation. Three to five slides is a starting range, not a quota. Do not reproduce every video as a redundant carousel, publish placeholders, or repeat evergreen introduction/library posts for each episode. Reuse suitable existing posts and link them where supported.
- Adapt the assets to each channel's actual supported placements and controls. Instagram Stories/Highlights do not imply equivalent features elsewhere. Use existing connected accounts only; a video publication instruction does not authorize new service connections, paid tools, advertisements or crossposting to another account.

## Visual direction

Use a polished educational-publisher style: confident typography, generous margins, one clear visual per panel, and concise copy. Preserve a recognizable family across Story, carousel and video while giving each topic its own restrained, rich colour. Deep teal, blue, plum, evergreen and terracotta are useful examples; warm ivory #F5F1E8, charcoal #171A1C and sparse muted gold #B89554 can anchor the system. Avoid excessive gold, tiny text, decorative clutter and luxury claims. Use strong text contrast and proper Bengali shaping.

Use the actual complete Earnalism website-header logo-and-wordmark from an authorized original; preserve its proportions and all lettering. Never redraw it with generated text, substitute an icon-only mark, stretch it or upscale a profile thumbnail as an original. Place it on a suitable quiet panel where needed. Verify the current public source rather than assuming a cached asset is current. The observed source was `https://theearnalism.com/assets/brand/earnalism-brand-lockup.png`.

Story 1080×1920 and feed carousel 1080×1350 are useful starting canvases, not universal platform specifications. Compose independently for their crop and interface overlays. Reserve a dedicated clear region for the native link sticker; the visible printed call-to-action is not a clickable link. Check every panel at realistic phone size, including the longest body line, full logo, book-cover attribution and sticker bounds. Do not split one large image across grid posts.

Use verified edition details and actual rights-cleared book covers for library tie-ins. Attribute authors accurately and disclose access requirements where relevant. Do not invent titles, audio availability, library size, testimonials, awards or scientific claims. Reuse current primary research for scientific statements; source new claims in the caption or final slide. Include #earnalism #learnalism together in companion post captions, with relevant topic tags rather than keyword blocks.

## Approval and publishing

Include finished companions, captions, destinations and intended accounts in the existing consolidated exact-candidate release review, clearly labelled PROPOSED — NOT LIVE. Reuse explicit approval for unchanged items; this skill update alone grants no future public-posting authority. A channel without publishing authority receives a ready handoff. Missing optional companions must not block an otherwise qualified video.

Verify the signed-in target account before changes. Publish through supported connectors or the documented connected-browser workflow and the live normal interface. Existing Meta Business Suite may be used when it already manages the intended account. Inspect the actual Story composer for link support and selected destinations; do not assume a Facebook selection also authorizes Instagram or vice versa. If a native feature is unavailable, provide the exact asset/link and a mobile handoff instead of connecting services or bypassing controls.

Upload only the final ordered slides and approved caption. Inspect accepted aspect ratio, crop and every slide before Share. For Stories, inspect each item's actual image and link separately; batch processing can temporarily hide edit controls or change their order. Target the item's own controls rather than a global numbered Edit button. Size and place the native sticker after inspecting its rendered bounds; do not reuse old drag coordinates or infer the last item's placement from the first. Keep essential text clear.

Publish once, then verify before another submission. A toast or composer preview is insufficient. Reopen the public carousel and check every slide and saved caption. Open each live Story, inspect the sticker placement and verify its actual destination. Record public IDs/URLs, asset hashes, date, account, genuine screenshots and any unverified condition separately from proposed previews. Avoid duplicate public QA uploads.

## Highlights, pins and corrections

Add approved Stories to relevant populated Highlights only when authorized: Start Here for brand orientation, Science for accurate lesson examples, and other sections only when actual content supports them. Verify playback, membership and covers. Never create empty Highlights, open private archives unnecessarily, or claim an approved icon cover was applied when the interface accepted only a Story-image crop. If custom cover upload is unavailable, retain the current cover and give a precise mobile handoff.

Preserve existing pins, avatar, category, website and profile settings unless separately authorized. Companion publishing does not authorize deleting or replacing older posts. For an explicitly approved replacement, verify the new post and all slides first, then perform only the specified old-post action through supported controls; distinguish archive from delete and preserve the old-to-new record.

If a published Story has a defect, inspect whether in-place editing is supported. In the observed Suite workflow Edit Story was disabled: correction needed replacement, so the corrected asset/link remained staged pending specific replacement authority. Do not repeatedly retry, silently delete/repost or publish a duplicate. Check any replacement's Highlight membership and link afterward. A prepared correction is not a live repair.

## Learn without overstating growth

Use the existing growth-feedback workflow for available native Story views/reach, link taps, exits, carousel saves/shares and attributable video visits/watch behaviour. Retain exact metric definitions, denominators and comparable publication ages; unavailable attribution stays unknown. A more attractive design or higher public view count alone does not prove that the companion caused growth. Carry supported creative lessons into the next release without making analytics collection a new approval gate.
