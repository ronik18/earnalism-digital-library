## Practical execution features

- Prepare a thumbnail-sized first frame before expensive animation: the object, conflict and intended result must be understandable without a title-card paragraph. Keep essential scientific labels; remove text that merely repeats narration or captions.
- Assign each beat a causal action (compare, track, reveal, transform, test). Use a new shot when information changes, not an arbitrary cut quota. Label time compression, schematic scales and imaginary cutaways when their literal reading would mislead.
- Script speech as complete natural thought groups. Preserve the accepted voice and natural pace; do not time-stretch speech to fit a band. Mark contrast, emphasis, rests and a quiet reveal. A robotic-delivery claim needs actual listening evidence, not views or an automated score.
- Keep one recognisable learning promise across the pilot. Lead public copy with the specific puzzle; put source/disclosure detail in the appropriate saved fields, remain concise and truthful, and preserve required #earnalism #learnalism. Avoid stacking a science follow request, generic library sales pitch and comment request in the same short.
- Check actual recommendation/restriction status before prescribing a setting change. Do not remove required or correctly applied AI labels as a reach tactic; assess actual disclosure policy and any erroneous-label correction through existing authority. Do not weaken reuse/privacy choices, add unrelated hashtags, buy reach or repeatedly repost as a growth fix. Existing eligibility is not guaranteed distribution.
- Complete ordinary full audiovisual review and destination-safe composition checks; inspect transitions as well as stills. If a sampled decode shows a tiled/corrupt frame, distinguish source, decoder and platform playback before changing media. A growth study does not waive a confirmed delivery defect.
