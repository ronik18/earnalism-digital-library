# Topic-relevant hashtags by platform

Owner rule: include **#earnalism #learnalism together** in every published YouTube video/Short description and Instagram Reel/post caption. Preserve these exact spellings. This does not authorize publishing, editing old posts, new services or spending.

## Select for fit, not a promised viral multiplier

At packaging time, identify the actual subject, viewer question, language and intended audience. Shortlist only tags that accurately describe the finished content. Check relevant native suggestions/search results when available; inspect meaning and context, not merely the largest post count. Trending, popular or suggested does not mean a tag will increase this video's distribution. Do not claim a tag is tested, unrestricted or currently trending without dated evidence. A missing search surface is a data limitation, not a release blocker.

Keep the mandatory brand pair, then choose the smallest useful set of specific topic and audience/category tags. Remove duplicates, misleading translations, unrelated seasonal/geographic tags, engagement bait and generic #viral/#fyp/#explorepage fillers. A bilingual library brand does not make every English science episode Bengali content. Do not fabricate hashtag-volume estimates or use a permanent copied tag bank across unrelated subjects.

## Instagram Reels and posts

Use natural topic terms in the caption as well as a few directly relevant hashtags. **Provisional operating default: at most five total, including the two brand tags**; fewer is fine. This conservative default reflects the reported five-tag rollout, not a proven optimal count or a claim that every account exposes the same control. Respect the live composer's actual limit; if it is lower, retain both brand tags and reduce optional tags. Do not bypass a restriction by stuffing comments. Recheck official/current account guidance when a material discrepancy occurs.

Template: `#earnalism #learnalism #[SpecificMechanism] #[RelevantSubject] [optional precise audience/category]`.
Example for evaporation, not a universal default: `#earnalism #learnalism #Evaporation #WaterCycle #EverydayScience`.

## YouTube landscape and Shorts

Keep the searchable title readable; place the brand pair and a small relevant topic set in the description. YouTube can suggest hashtags and choose up to three to display near the title; do not promise which will display or confuse these with Studio's internal Tags field. More than 60 hashtags causes YouTube to ignore all hashtags; this is a policy ceiling, never a target. Misleading/unrelated tags can trigger removal. #Shorts is not required to make a qualifying video a Short; add a format tag only when it adds useful accurate context.

Template: `#earnalism #learnalism #[SpecificTopic] [optional relevant series/category]`.
Example: `#earnalism #learnalism #Evaporation #EverydayScience`.

## Fast pre-publication check and learning loop

- Confirm the exact brand pair, unique tags, topic/language fit and current composer acceptance. Verify the saved public caption/description after an authorized publication; a filled form is not proof.
- Record the chosen set, platform, selection rationale and date with the episode's packaging. No private account figures go into reusable skill files.
- Treat hashtag changes as a low-cost secondary experiment after the hook, explanation and packaging. Keep the brand pair fixed; vary only the optional set when feasible. Use the existing 24-hour, 7-day and 28-day review checkpoints, platform-specific reach/discovery and follower/subscriber conversion where exposed, with comparable format, length and publication age. Retention, saves and shares are guardrails; missing attribution stays unknown. A before/after difference is observational, not a randomized causal test.
- Adopt an optional tag pattern only after repeated relevant observations; revise or retire it when meaning, platform guidance or audience evidence changes. Do not repeatedly edit live metadata to chase noise or add an approval loop.
