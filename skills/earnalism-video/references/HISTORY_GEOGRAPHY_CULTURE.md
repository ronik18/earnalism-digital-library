# Historical, geographical and cultural production standard

Apply automatically when an Earnalism episode concerns history, geography, regional life, heritage, mythology or cultural traditions. This consolidates the demonstrated Diwali v17 qualities into future production decisions; it does not make the Diwali artwork, script, festival palette or experimental GPU settings universal templates.

## Brief and pictorial storyboard

For each important finding, record its source, evidence status, pictured subject/action and visible learning payoff in the existing storyboard. Distinguish documented history, attributed tradition and artistic reconstruction in both narration and images. Do not present generated illustration as archival evidence. For geography, verify place names, map geometry, scale, routes and boundaries against suitable authoritative sources; label uncertainty or disputed interpretations when material.

Choose visuals that explain the finding: a located map and route for movement, a readable timeline for chronology, a contextual reconstruction or manuscript detail for history, and people carrying out a culturally appropriate practice for traditions. Let the viewer see the relationship being explained rather than merely read a claim over a decorative illustration. Avoid unsupported dates, invented artifacts and generic icon substitutions.

## Attractive, purposeful presentation

- Open on an immediately readable, richly composed subject with a clear question or promise and an early visual payoff. Avoid delaying the lesson behind a long ident.
- Author consistent original illustrations with a strong focal subject, coherent costumes/architecture, depth, texture and deliberate light/colour contrast. Choose the palette for the place, period and emotion; Diwali's luminous gold/indigo is a festival example.
- Progress from context to meaningful action/detail to the visual answer. Use maps, reveals and explanatory motion where they teach. Decorative particles, camera drift and repeated pans cannot substitute for events or explanations.
- Compose landscape and portrait independently. Prefer native landscape source artwork and motion for wide human scenes, and native portrait compositions for Shorts. Check every important head, hand, prop and caption in the final crop and under platform interface overlays.
- Preserve the exact full Earnalism logo lockup, safe placement, actual-speech captions, restrained topic-appropriate music with rests, and the approved three-second ending. Use [visual storytelling](VISUAL_STORYTELLING.md), [editorial presentation](EDITORIAL_PRESENTATION.md) and [music direction](MUSIC_DIRECTION.md) for implementation.

## Natural action and interaction

Specify one readable action with a beginning, result and end, stable expressions/identity/gaze, and visible acting hands and contact points. Build enough space into source artwork for the complete movement before inference. For a bow, review the bend and return as well as palms and expression; for a family offering, review the offer and recipient response. Do not claim transfer or eating when the footage only shows an offer.

Review the raw clip, complete edited shot and phone-size delivery crop. Reject distracting anatomy, unstable faces, unclear contact or an unfinished requested action even when the render technically passes. Test action completion and strict pose adherence separately: v17 completed the homecoming bow but did not maintain joined palms throughout the return. Its family scene demonstrated an offering and appreciative response, not a handover. Preserve these limits rather than claiming perfect animation or requiring future scenes to reproduce those defects.

When the owner selects Wan2.2, follow [the isolated Colab workflow](WAN22_COLAB.md), its tested scene-bound profiles, job-bound input checks, budget forecasts and durable export safeguards. The demonstrated detail clips used 1280×704 source frames; a 1080p export does not create native 1080p detail. New scenes need their own reviewed artwork, actual compute authorization and visual qualification. Never reuse consumed allowances or start automatic paid retries.

## Voice, delivery and closure

Use the established [topic-based voice route](VOICE_PRODUCTION.md#topic-based-narrator-routing--owner-default-2026-11-07): Indian male narration for Indian regional/cultural/mythological content, accepted Voice B for other topics for now. Check new names and pronunciation in actual audio. Aditya's accepted Diwali recording does not qualify every future script or authorize provider usage. Preserve accepted audio on visual-only changes and verify synchronization separately in both compositions.

Deliver [H.264/AAC MP4 and source-bound PCM WAV masters](DELIVERY_FORMATS.md), retain actual source resolution and provenance, and qualify the exact final candidate. Define the concrete finish line before refinement and apply [finite refinement closure](CONTINUOUS_IMPROVEMENT.md#finite-refinement-closure): fix material defects within the authorized bounded pass, select a reviewed alternative when necessary, and freeze the completed candidate. Scores and GIF comparisons are editorial guidance, not moving acceptance thresholds or predictions of reach. Publishing remains a separate destination-specific owner decision.
