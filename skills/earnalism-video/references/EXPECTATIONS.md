## Intended experience

Earnalism is a premium learning platform. Build understanding with an original, sincere teacher-like delivery, visually explained mechanisms, a strong opening question and a closing visual callback. Aim for clarity, curiosity and meaningful recall rather than decoration, constant stimulation or therapeutic claims. Every topic needs its own researched explanation, diagram model, storyboard and musical decisions. Select a tested subject adapter only when its mechanism fits; a new subject otherwise needs new tested scenes and must not be made by relabeling an unrelated explanation.

## Non-negotiable acceptance

1. Research every spoken factual claim and visual assertion using authoritative/primary sources. Keep citations, retrieved evidence, uncertainty and claim coverage. No unresolved factual issue may publish.
2. Make animation explain cause, viewpoint, scale or change. Check label/disk/caption clearance and staged reading order in both actual compositions at narrow and fullscreen sizes. Inspect transitions and the complete brand ending; code or decode checks alone cannot certify visual quality.
3. Keep raw recognition, actual word times and hashes. Audio-bound alignment with edits=[] is insufficient if the current script has changed: raw/display tokens must still match the current script, allowing only the supported exact orthographic/number equivalences.
4. Check individual passages, paragraph/sentence transitions, body-score coverage, gentle body-edge fades, clean END handoff and encoded peaks (preserved source silence/re-entry for historical candidates) and the scored-versus-dry comparison. Retain every remaining flag. The selected voice prompt and conservative 122 WPM / 110–140 diagnostic screen are separate: the screen is not a generation target and must not be widened to hide selected-B flags.
5. Obtain real full-playback, headphone/speaker, naturalness/resemblance and first-time understanding feedback as applicable. Automated checks cannot supply those responses or prove a sixty-year-old impression, musical sophistication or enduring retention.
6. Bind final human approval to the exact delivery manifest. Changes invalidate affected evidence; preserve history and create fresh pending reviews. No self-score, generic 'auto-approve' instruction or successful tests grants human approval or clears a fixture guard.
