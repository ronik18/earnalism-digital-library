# Audience promise, packaging and measured improvement

Use when preparing a new episode, qualifying a production edition or evaluating real results. Preserve the factual, graphical, rights, voice and delivery gates in the main skill. A simulated viewer critique is an editorial hypothesis, not genuine viewer feedback or a prediction of clicks, completion, subscribers or revenue. Do not describe an imagined “top-three worldwide viewer” as a verified person or audience segment.

## Before generation: state the learning and viewing promise

Record the intended viewer, likely prior knowledge, one consequential misunderstanding, the complete promised payoff and why the topic belongs in Earnalism's series. Write the outcome as an observable explanation or prediction, not “premium,” “unforgettable” or “viral.” For Moon: explain phases using reflected sunlight and changing viewing geometry, distinguish quarter-cycle progress from a half-lit disk, and distinguish phases from eclipses. Audience planning does not determine YouTube's made-for-kids setting; review the actual intended audience and presentation at upload.

Give each format a purpose. Landscape is the primary mechanism-led explanation, with spatial relationships and a complete payoff. Independently composed Shorts can introduce one compelling misconception and answer it completely, or carry the same complete lesson when its duration supports that purpose. Do not assume a crop, shorter duration, duplicate title or incomplete teaser will work. Record which purpose this particular Short serves and how its captions, safe areas, opening and payoff support it.

Before narration, complete `audience-plan.json` with one truthful selected title **per format** (alternatives are optional), the title/thumbnail relationship, the opening visual event, an application/retrieval decision (including a reason when unnecessary) and an honest next-step status. The creator makes ordinary editorial choices autonomously from the topic and evidence; do not hand the owner a blank planning form. Each hypothesis identifies the curiosity or search intent it tests; do not promise an experiment that has not been run. Choose a title consistent with the actual script and graphic qualifications. Keep the selection reason and any alternatives actually considered; do not publish several copies merely to test titles. Non-fixture job construction and execution require a valid plan before model setup; a pending plan is not permission to consume compute.

## Learn from real results without chasing noise

Plan configurable checkpoints, initially 48 hours, 7 days and 28 days **after actual publication**. These are observation windows, not algorithm deadlines or a request to schedule background automation. Record publication time, checkpoint coverage, reporting lag, format and traffic source. Keep missing/unavailable values as unknown and verify current metric definitions in official Analytics guidance/UI before comparisons.

For landscape, examine impressions and click-through rate where reported, discovery source/search terms, watch time, average view duration/percentage and retention around the opening, mechanism and payoff. For Shorts, examine the current feed exposure/view-versus-swipe and engaged-view measures where available, duration/percentage watched and the retention curve. Keep Shorts feed results separate from browse/search CTR. Interpret loops and platform view definitions explicitly; do not compare unlike denominators or traffic mixes.

Record subscribers gained, returning-viewer evidence, real comprehension feedback and relevant next-lesson/library actions when the existing authorized system actually measures them. Only record revenue or conversion attribution that exists; views do not imply earnings. Report counts, denominators, sample size and limits. A small early result is descriptive evidence, not causal proof or grounds for a guaranteed growth claim.

At each checkpoint choose one evidence-backed question: packaging mismatch, weak early payoff, explanatory confusion, delivery friction or an unclear next step. Preserve the baseline and change history. A retention dip does not identify its cause without context; replay the corresponding content and use real viewer explanations. Prefer one coherent revision or next-episode experiment over continuous speculative polishing.

## Supported local commands

From the project root, the creator can initialize a fresh prepared folder, complete its plan, then run:

```sh
python3 video_studio/audience_plan.py generation /absolute/new-candidate
python3 video_studio/audience_plan.py package /absolute/new-candidate
python3 video_studio/production_journal.py summary /absolute/new-candidate
python3 video_studio/audience_plan.py handoff-check /absolute/new-candidate --target youtube
```

`studio.prepare` initializes the planning files automatically; `audience_plan.py init FOLDER` supports a fresh older draft without overwriting existing planning. A filled Moon-only planning input is `examples/moon-audience-episode.json` in the software root. It is an example, not a completed new video. After editing a plan, update the packaging’s plan SHA and selection deliberately. Render emits private format metadata and a pending handoff; changing packaging afterward requires a fresh bound candidate. Do not run a costly render merely to obtain a filled form.

For `production_journal.py work FOLDER RECORD.json`, use event_id, activity, actual_human_minutes and actual_compute_minutes (unknown may be null); optional actual_compute_units, actual_cost/currency and budget remain distinct. `observe` requires an actual platform ID, timestamp/period, format/checkpoint/traffic source, observed basis, denominator/context, metrics and hashed export evidence. A local binding validates provenance, not external data authenticity. No journal result proves channel monetization eligibility.
