# Evidence reconciliation and diagnostic pilots

Use when incorporating external research/model reviews or preparing a prospective growth pilot. This supplements existing production and growth references; it adds no permission, routine study or release approval.

## Durable safeguards

- Bind every performance observation to platform and post ID where applicable, with recorded visibility and report scope; include creative version and format where applicable. Titles are non-unique; do not join observations by title alone. If identity conflicts, preserve the original record and quarantine the affected summary until a source-bound correction is documented.
- A reproducible calculation can still use a wrong identity. Validate joins and denominators separately from arithmetic. Distinguish native post timestamps, exact first-public visibility, capture age and metric reporting cutoff.
- A model must state which actual files, frames and audio it accessed. Estimated visual timestamps are not synchronized AV inspection. If required attachments cannot be inspected, label that review incomplete and supply readable extracts; reported validation is not an independent rerun. Model agreement or a high-performing post does not establish listening quality, learning, causality or revenue.
- Keep raw account data and dated performance in the project evidence, outside reusable skill defaults. Preserve original reports, snapshots and corrections as separate versions.
- Maintain existing factual/graphical, caption, rights, listening/AV and delivery checks, accepted voice source, exact watermark/ending, required paired hashtags and one consolidated final release decision. Native platform labels and truthful diagram qualifications remain separate from added brand slogans.

## Provisional editorial defaults

- Make the subject and specific question understandable in the first visible scene. Let animation resolve a causal relationship; retain labels that carry meaning, remove redundant prose only when reading/listening completeness survives.
- Choose the next concept by source support, distinct learning value, actual model/assets/audio availability and permitted effort. Popular-topic resemblance and review-model preference do not establish audience demand.
- Preserve natural narration and sufficient explanation time. Runtime targets, text cuts and fixed closing lengths are hypotheses, never automatic percentages or rules.
- Use one appropriate series invitation after value. State a library offer only when the destination really provides it. Portrait needs its own composition, overlay/crop checks and saved cover/caption verification.
- Check official source wording and dates when a real delivery question arises. Conflicting Help pages or changing UI require an explicit uncertainty and actual authorized delivery verification, not a permanent guessed platform rule.

## Experiment-only choices and feedback

- If matched historical windows/denominators are absent, label the next release a diagnostic pilot. A single creative cross-posted twice is one creative observed in different platform environments, not two randomized tests. Organic releases of different creatives/topics are not randomized A/B tests; matching length, text count or capture age does not isolate a treatment without a documented assignment/control design.
- Before a diagnostic-pilot release, record one audience hypothesis, selected treatment, one primary native metric per platform, unavailable fields, quality/conversion guardrails and expected observation checkpoints. Do not invent a growth threshold from tiny historical counts.
- Capture actual age, native cutoff, raw counts and definitions near 24h, 72h and 7d if later authorized; preserve processing revisions and missed observations. These are checkpoints, not scheduled tasks by default.
- Keep follow/subscriber outcomes, exposure, initial choice, subsequent watch behavior and commerce separate. YouTube Stayed to watch is an initial-choice measure; Instagram Viewers is not missing Reach. Net subscriber change is not gross acquisition.
- Retain sparse or mixed outcomes as inconclusive. Revise a demonstrated quality defect immediately; repeat a promising hypothesis with another independently useful creative before elevating it to a default. Retire advice for repeated harm, changed policy/definition, or better evidence. Optional experiments do not hold an otherwise qualified ordinary release.
