# Platform playbooks: useful attention, return visits and attributable value

Use for future episode briefs, packaging and requested growth reviews. Read `PLATFORM_GROWTH.md` for measurement/revenue boundaries and `INSTAGRAM_GROWTH.md` for discovery/profile claims. These playbooks refine production choices; they add no release gate, publication permission or compulsory form. The optional `templates/growth-brief.template.json` records an experiment when useful; null fields never block ordinary generation.

At a new brief, reuse the selected project's `evidence/current-growth-review.json` when present. Read its current diagnosis and applicable decision, not every historical report. Keep account-specific counts in dated project evidence. Apply supported craft defaults; carry a growth hypothesis into the next suitable test without calling it a proven rule. Refresh analytics for a requested audit or an available due review, not before every render. The same topic may need different packaging and scope on Reels, Shorts and landscape.

## Shared editorial contract

- State one audience, familiar question and new understanding. The digital-library connection should be relevant, truthful and available; do not imply learning guarantees income.
- Prefer topics supported by actual audience questions, search/retention evidence or recurring misconceptions. If demand is inferred editorially, label it a hypothesis.
- Favor a connected cluster whose episodes each add a distinct mechanism or application. Do not turn a successful scene into interchangeable scripts for unrelated topics.
- Show the subject immediately. Plan an early visible answer to the opening promise, then develop the explanation; exact seconds depend on the material.
- Use observed puzzle → prediction/demonstration → inference → application/payoff where appropriate. Resolve the promised question rather than repeatedly withholding it.
- Explanatory animation must make causal relationships understandable, preserve scientific meaning and stage essential labels with narration. Colour and characters may vary by topic.
- More elaborate animation, a save, repeated viewing or reported confidence does not itself prove understanding or growth. Retain material qualifications; use one useful prediction or transfer question when it serves the lesson. This is a design/learning safeguard, not a compulsory audience study or extra release gate.
- Keep B's accepted vocal identity and source audio. Growth advice alone does not authorize retakes, pitch/tempo edits or replacement providers. Natural momentum permits purposeful variation.
- Keep rights-cleared music below intelligibility, with deliberate phrasing and silence. Louder music, more cuts and faster speech are not evidence of stronger engagement.
- Preserve the exact approved emblem/watermark and ending, no added tagline, original notebooks and production records. A platform-generated label is distinct from an added brand slogan.
- Retain synchronized actual-audio captions and transcript/source access. Design for interface masks, profile crops and narrow players; do not hide factual qualifications to look premium.
- One relevant invitation after useful value. Preserve **#earnalism #learnalism** together, plus only relevant topic terms/tags within the actual composer limit.
- Authority remains scoped: no account-setting changes, messages, paid distribution, uploads or publication merely because a strategy recommends them.

Priorities are conditional, not a revenue forecast. Fix a real delivery defect before optional creative tests. Tiny samples cannot identify a reliable winner.

## Instagram Reels

**Content and opening**

- Begin with a recognizable daily experience and one truthful tension the viewer can see. Make the first frame useful in the scrolling feed; the profile cover is a different surface.
- Offer an insight worth saving or sending because it helps someone understand or act safely. Do not manufacture urgency, authority, testimonials or extraordinary numerical results.
- Write one opening alternative only when testing it answers a real question; object-first versus question-first is a hypothesis, not a mandatory template pair.

**Story, visual design, voice and length**

- Give each scene visible explanatory progress. A rehook should open a genuinely new connection after delivering value, not impose an arbitrary interruption quota.
- Compose vertical scenes independently. Keep object motion, crucial labels and captions clear of actual Instagram interface areas; inspect the processed upload rather than assume a local layout applied.
- Use subject-appropriate colour/texture with stable semantic meaning. Decorative visual density must not displace reading time or the mechanism.
- Choose length by the learning job. A concise complete insight and a fuller explanation are distinct options; compare useful watch seconds, proportional viewing and conversion together.
- An average-watch-time/length ratio is descriptive, not a measured completion probability. Replays and denominator definitions matter.

**Cover, caption, discoverability and invitation**

- Make a dedicated cover with one focal relationship and short readable copy; inspect current profile/feed crops and the saved public cover. Avoid accidental old/tagline-bearing assets.
- Caption: truthful puzzle/benefit first; concise explanation/context second; one appropriate next step; sources/disclosure where needed; paired brand hashtags and relevant topic tags.
- Use natural topic nouns in the caption, profile and meaningful on-screen labels. Keyword blocks, unrelated hashtags and exact-count “viral presets” have no justified role.
- A follow CTA should name the recurring learning value. A save/share CTA should fit the content; choose one, not all. Do not automate comment-keyword DMs or unsolicited outreach.
- The profile should make its reading, selected-audiobook and visual-learning offer clear. Proposed pins should demonstrate the promise and lead to a real next lesson; do not overhaul a profile for every release.

**Cadence, distribution and measurement**

- Start from capacity. Two qualified episodes per week is an initial **capacity hypothesis**, not a scheduled commitment, optimum or publication authority. Reduce frequency before reducing primary quality.
- Select practical windows from actual follower activity when available; otherwise test two declared audience-local windows. Record timezone, topic mix and extra promotion; pooled “best times” are weak priors.
- Authorized useful Story questions, native reposts or genuine collaborations can extend discovery. Changing download/remix/embed/search permissions requires its own scope; there is no blanket “enable everything” growth switch.
- Record views/plays and unique reach separately; capture non-follower reach, watch time/skip/retention, shares, saves, follows and profile activity only where exposed.
- Diagnose shares/reach, saves/reach and follows/reach using actual counts and matching scopes. Combined shares are not necessarily private sends; blank metrics stay unknown.
- If native desktop Insights exposes Viewers but not Reach or retention, retain those labels and limitations. Do not invent a follow-per-reach winner, reconstruct an average-watch-time denominator, or explain every low-view Reel as an opening failure.
- Ordinary Reels and Trial Reels are different cohorts. A Trial Reel reaches non-followers and is **external distribution**, not private review. Use only with publication authority; leave auto-share off unless expressly authorized.
- Trial performance is not a randomized A/B result. Per-Reel follower attribution is distinct from total account follower change; aggregate profile clicks cannot establish a Reel-specific purchase funnel.

## YouTube Shorts

**Content, opening and story**

- Deliver one complete insight: concrete puzzle → visible mechanism → answer/payoff. The original core lesson may be shared with landscape while portrait composition and interface clearance remain independent.
- Show the subject immediately; align the first words and action. Avoid a logo pre-roll and long unexplained setup. Preserve the approved end ident and actual narration timing.
- Keep meaningful emphasis and thinking space. Do not treat faster, shorter, louder, artificial loops or a high replay ratio as inherently better.
- Choose the shortest version that preserves the insight; a technical maximum duration is not a recommendation or reason to fill the limit.

**Packaging, captions and journey**

- Title: specific truthful curiosity or useful question, important subject early. Cover/frame: readable subject and accurate promise for profile/search surfaces; it cannot repair a weak feed opening.
- Use native English captions or the reviewed burned-caption delivery strategy without accidental double layers. Verify processed audio, captions, cover and disclosure settings.
- Description: concise learning promise, relevant sources/context, one next lesson/library route and **#earnalism #learnalism**. Tags mainly help spelling variants; do not keyword-stuff.
- Use the actual Related video control for an available complementary destination with verified ID. If both formats contain the same explanation, call it a format choice, not extra depth.
- A related-video link enables a journey; it does not prove cross-format conversion. Publish a private destination only with the existing final release authority.
- Treat a successful Short as a complete learning experience. A linked landscape version needs a truthful reason to choose that format or added depth when it actually exists; never promise a fuller lesson that is the same explanation. Cross-format watching is possible, not assured.

**Cadence, distribution and measurement**

- There is no minimum posting quota for recommendation. Reuse the capacity hypothesis, not a claim that daily posting unlocks reach.
- Separate feed choice (**Stayed to watch**) from continuation (**engaged AVD/AVP**). Strong conditional AVP does not cancel a weak opening choice.
- Track engaged views, watch time, shares, subscribers gained/lost and actual next-video movement where measurable. Keep public starts, exposure, unique viewers and qualified views distinct.
- Match Shorts by topic family, duration band, organic/paid source and public age. Do not compare a short looping clip and a long explanation solely by completion percentage.
- Native title/thumbnail A/B is not available for Shorts; repeated manual changes or different uploads are not a randomized test.

## YouTube short landscape explainers

- Preserve landscape as the primary format. The existing approximate 90-second production default remains an operating starting point, not a proven growth optimum.
- Build the title and thumbnail as complementary promises: a specific question/useful surprise, one focal relationship and readable short copy; brand/episode suffix after the important words.
- Differentiate Search intent from Home curiosity. Use actual audience/search evidence where available; accurate descriptions support discovery without guaranteeing ranking.
- Match the promise immediately and deliver an early explanatory payoff. Stage causal motion and labels; avoid dense slide text that duplicates the narration/captions.
- Diagnose registered impressions and source-specific CTR together with retention and watch time. Use watch time from impressions per registered impression if available; do not multiply unmatched all-source AVD by source-specific CTR.
- A low CTR on tiny impressions is not a proven packaging defect. Different source/audience mixes can change CTR even when creative is unchanged.
- Where eligible, prefer native concurrent title/thumbnail A/B, with materially different truthful alternatives and the actual watch-time outcome. Private videos and Shorts are excluded; do not publish just to unlock testing.
- Accept Winner, Performed Same or Inconclusive as reported. Do not stop early because one option temporarily leads, change variants mid-test or describe sequential swaps as randomized evidence.
- Offer one actual next lesson via a relevant permitted end screen/playlist. Clear the diagram and captions; do not add empty closing time or promise deeper material when it is the same explanation.
- Preserve reviewed cover/caption/source/disclosure and audience choices through the existing upload handoff; local metadata files do not prove settings were applied.

## YouTube deeper landscape videos

- Expand only when the question earns additional depth: demonstration, causal model, misconception, evidence and useful application. Do not pad a short answer into an advertising length.
- Structure sections around questions with visible payoffs. Use concise bridges, prediction opportunities and appropriately paced diagrams; graphics should explain what words alone cannot show.
- Test scope/length as a distinct content hypothesis, preserving the established voice and factual/rights safeguards. There is no universal ideal length.
- Judge both minutes watched and percentage viewed against similar-length, similarly sourced content; inspect specific drops before editing.
- A complete short may invite the fuller treatment, but do not withhold its answer. Cross-format recommendation is possible; actual viewer switching must be measured.
- Use earned demand, sustainable costs and actual eligible receipts to decide further investment. Ad eligibility, payout and a growing audience are different outcomes.

## Lean craft check before the existing final decision

Fold this into ordinary review; do not create another approval form or gate:

- Shared: clear viewer/promise; original accurate mechanism; immediate subject; meaningful early progress; complete payoff; accepted B/music/branding; readable synchronized captions; rights/source access.
- Instagram: dedicated crop-safe cover; useful caption with natural topic language; one CTA; paired hashtags; processed caption/audio check; ordinary versus authorized Trial exposure explicit.
- Shorts: opening words/action agree; vertical interface clearance; caption strategy avoids duplication; accurate cover/title; Related video targets the correct available lesson.
- Landscape: small browse title/thumbnail promise agrees with the opening; narrow-player labels clear captions; one relevant next lesson; any native A/B is eligible and authorized.
- Deeper work: extra time buys substantive explanation; no filler, artificial suspense or revenue-driven padding.
- Record known missing analytics as unknown and continue normal production when primary checks pass. Optional growth work never excuses factual, graphical, caption, rights or audible failures.

## Feedback and adaptation

- Retain a dated snapshot with exact asset/account, publication age, duration, source mix, metric cutoff/definition and numerator/denominator. Realtime estimates are not settled revenue or retention.
- Compare matched 7-day outcomes; revisit evergreen performance at 28 days. Early 24/72-hour checks identify delivery problems and hypotheses, not automatic winner/loser verdicts.
- Each controlled test names one main change, a primary native outcome, practical effect of interest and quality/conversion guardrails before publication. Balance topic and distribution as far as feasible.
- Review weekly; adopt provisionally only after repeated useful matched improvement, or a native conclusive test. Sparse events remain inconclusive; no universal follower/CTR/retention benchmark.
- Reconsider every four weeks, or immediately when a verified quality/policy defect appears. Revise or retire a rule for repeated harm, no useful lift, changed metric definitions or better evidence; preserve the baseline/history.
- Keep growth, satisfied learning, return behavior, relevant library visits and attributable receipts/costs separate. No advice can guarantee millions/billions of views or income.
