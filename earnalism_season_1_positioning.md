# Earnalism Season 1 Positioning

## Positioning Line

Earnalism Season 1 is a premium listening room for Bengali and English classics.

## Core Promise

Reader-first classics, beautiful graphical covers, and natural quality-gated narration only when production evidence proves the audiobook is ready.

## Audience Segments

- Bengali readers and diaspora families who want dignified Bengali classics in a calm modern interface.
- Students and teachers who need trustworthy public-domain classics without noisy reading experiences.
- English short-classic readers who respond to gothic, comic, and human short fiction.
- Early subscribers who value a curated literary institution more than a content warehouse.

## Proof Points

- 229-book launch inventory scored for release feasibility and market fit.
- Bengali and English shelves are separated by release truth, not generic popularity.
- First Bengali audiobook is live with approved section-following narration.
- A Ghost Story is approved and asset-backed; final paid-ad route check remains required before spend.
- Audiobooks appear only after narration, sync, metadata, endpoint, and browser gates pass.

## Trust Copy

Earnalism does not publish every generated narration. Listening releases appear only after quality gates pass. Reader editions remain available and intentional while audio is prepared.

## Bengali Positioning

Bengali classics should feel native, literary, and respected. Bengali reader-only titles are not incomplete products; they are premium reading editions with audio intentionally hidden until narration passes.

## English Classics Positioning

English classics should feel editorial and atmospheric, with short classics used for discovery and long-form classics used as SEO and authority anchors.

## Audiobook Positioning

Use “quality-gated narration,” “approved audiobook,” “natural voice narration,” and “section-following narration” where evidence supports them.

## Reader-Only Positioning

Use “reader edition live,” “premium reading room,” “classic ready to read tonight,” and “audio appears only after release gates pass.”

## Do-Not-Say List

- Do not say all Tier 1 books are audiobooks yet.
- Do not say “full audiobook library live.”
- Do not say word-level sync for paragraph/stanza sync.
- Do not say AI-generated audiobook if it weakens trust.
- Do not advertise unapproved audio.
- Do not use raw slugs in customer-facing copy.
