# Owner direction: expressive, natural audiobook narration

Owner instruction, 31 October 2026: “Keep a close eye on the naturalness and non-robotic texture on the audiobook voiceovers. There should be no mistake, no loophole, - there should only be unforgettable listener experience full of emotional expressions, attractive story-telling voice and attitude in the tone in general in every single newly generated audiobooks.”

Apply this direction to every newly generated audiobook in the campaign. The release outcome is an engaging, memorable listening experience with exact content fidelity. Voice labels and successful generation jobs do not establish that outcome.

Choose and audition a voice against the actual book: language, genre, narrative perspective, atmosphere, emotional changes, dialogue and difficult names. Require natural phrasing, varied but coherent cadence, a stable human-sounding texture, expressive delivery and an engaging storytelling attitude appropriate to the passage. Reflective, documentary and restrained passages must remain contextually credible; do not force theatrical emotion or constant intensity. Ryan, Girija and other licensed candidates require actual representative audio evidence for the selected edition.

Review complete narration and transitions through distinct production, critical review, repair and release verification stages. Cover opening, dialogue, emotional shifts, dense or difficult passages, and ending in representative review; one isolated sample never qualifies a full title. Check every segment and adjacent join for context, exact words, pronunciation, rhythm, pauses, expression, intelligibility, stable voice and acoustic consistency. A passing aggregate transcript score never clears a suspected missing, substituted, repeated or invented word.

Robotic texture, mechanical cadence, list-reading rhythm, choppy joins, fallback TTS, material flat delivery, forced expression or distracting artifacts block release until resolved. Preserve the current applicable rubric and stricter title-specific constraints; do not replace them with invented scores or lower standards to meet cost/time targets. Mark unavailable or unperformed checks UNVERIFIED. A short automated audio review is not human listening or whole-book acceptance, and must be labelled with its exact scope and confidence.

Repair only demonstrated defects using an authorized compatible voice/model/settings combination and rerun affected content/acoustic checks plus both adjacent transitions. Preserve healthy originals and their hashes. Keep retries bounded and respect consumed allocation/take limits. An inconclusive review does not authorize speculative repair, synthesis, another allocation, or release.

Use only appropriately licensed music where it strengthens the work; keep speech consistently intelligible with restrained level, ducking and smooth fades. Silence is appropriate where accompaniment weakens the story. Music cannot mask narration defects.

Publish only after all actual rights, fidelity, narration, timing, storage, source-version and production-player/customer-access gates pass, with no known material defects remaining. This direction does not assert perfect certainty, fabricate listening evidence, authorize unbounded spending, change source books, or authorize new synthesis/allocation in the current no-synthesis continuation.
