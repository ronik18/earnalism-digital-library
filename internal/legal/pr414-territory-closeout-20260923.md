## Radharani source layer

The underlying work by Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay is assessed separately from the 1940-edition Bengali Wikisource transcription/editorial layer. The [identified source page](https://bn.wikisource.org/wiki/রাধারাণী_(১৯৪০)) identifies the author, edition, editors and contributors' history; its footer links [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The public Book Detail Details panel now credits and links the source and contributors, identifies the license and its disclaimer, notes Earnalism's Reader reformatting, and applies ShareAlike only to a copyrightable adaptation of that reused layer. It does not purport to license separate covers, branding or code. This is attribution compliance for the source layer, not a claim that the public-domain underlying novel became newly copyrighted.

For US restoration of the underlying literary work, [17 USC §104A(h)(6)(B)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=(title:17%20section:104a%20edition:prelim)) requires a restored work **not** to have been in the public domain in its source country through expiration of term. The already-evidenced author death in 1894 and Indian expired-term result mean that condition fails for the underlying work at the 1996 restoration date. The Wikisource contribution/license question is separate.

The sign-in and selected Reader preferences are linked to requested functions. Home caches, tour memory and optional prompt memory need a purpose-specific UK storage/access assessment or removal before claiming UK storage compliance; no blanket `UK_STORAGE_COMPLIANT` conclusion is made here. The [ICO storage/access guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-the-exceptions/) limits the strictly-necessary exception to what is essential for the service the person requests. Nonessential analytics/advertising are disabled for the initial build; no decorative consent banner is added.

## UK representative

The initial product provides ongoing account authentication and reading-position processing, not merely isolated/occasional personal-data processing. Removing optional analytics does not make that recurring processing occasional. Under the [ICO's UK-representative test](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/receiving-personal-information-from-the-eea/), the occasional/low-risk/no-large-scale-sensitive-data exception cannot presently be claimed. If Earnalism intentionally offers Reader service to UK individuals without a UK establishment, appointment of an actual UK representative and publication of its contact details is the remaining external prerequisite. No representative is inferred or invented. UK Reader stays denied by the India-only accepted rights registry and signed proxy.

## US state privacy scope

Optional sale/sharing, targeted advertising, profiling, public UGC and unfinished commerce are outside the initial configuration. The [California Privacy Protection Agency](https://cppa.ca.gov/faq) states the CCPA `business` thresholds: preceding-year gross revenue at least $26.625 million, buying/selling/sharing personal information of at least 100,000 California residents/households, or at least half of annual revenue from sale/sharing. The latter two are not established by this no-sale/no-sharing configuration; the preceding-year revenue fact is not in the repository. Other state laws have different resident-processing thresholds, and no actual US processing volume is evidenced here. Therefore US state-law nonapplicability is **not** asserted and US Reader is not enabled by this checkpoint. This does not reopen the separate title-copyright findings.
