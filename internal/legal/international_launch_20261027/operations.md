# Minimal manual privacy and consumer operations

LOCAL PROPOSED PROCEDURE — assign an accountable operator and validate provider/legal retention requirements before publishing the corresponding promises. This file creates no scheduled job or automatic deletion.

Use existing sales@reoenterprise.org and admin contact intake. Do not collect passwords, card credentials or excess identity documents. Verify account ownership proportionately before access/export/deletion. Restrict records and exports to authorized operators.

1. Log receipt, request category, applicable jurisdiction/deadline and operator. Acknowledge consumer complaints within 48 hours and address within one month under applicable Indian e-commerce rules. For privacy requests, track the relevant statutory deadline; GDPR's normal one-month response period applies where in scope, with any permitted extension explained on time.
2. For access/correction, inspect actual account, contact, newsletter, reading and payment-reference records. Keep exports secure. Do not expose another person's information or provider credentials.
3. For erasure, inventory relevant collections and processor copies. Delete/de-identify data no longer needed; retain only records with documented legal/security/dispute grounds and a review date. Do not silently erase wallet balances, ledger events, payment records, accepted publication evidence or financial obligations. Obtain appropriate authority for any consequential customer-data operation. Explain partial retention to the requester.
4. Keep active account/reading state only while serving the account's continuity purpose. Review retention after an account closes. Support data is retained only while needed for the issue, related disputes and legal claims. Tax/payment records follow the accountant's applicable statutory period. Backups expire under the verified provider schedule; do not claim instant backup deletion. Establish actual region, access and deletion procedures with providers before international launch.
5. For an unsubscribe email, stop marketing use promptly and record the minimum necessary suppression entry; do not send further marketing pending resolution. A new intentional resubscription must be verified. Existing newsletter export UI is not permission for an unqualified campaign. Include a working unsubscribe mechanism and operator postal identity in outgoing campaigns; verify marketing laws for their recipient territories. Sending campaigns is outside this implementation.
6. Investigate a suspected incident, preserve restricted evidence, contain exposure and assess applicable notification duties. Do not announce an incident, leak logs or send third-party notices without the appropriate facts and authority.
7. For failed delivery, duplicate payment or refund requests, reconcile the provider reference with actual delivery and ledger evidence. Use existing qualified refund tooling; never simulate a successful refund, alter historical financial evidence or cancel an entitlement without scope. Record the customer remedy and bank/provider processing time.

Business facts still required: named grievance officer and accountable privacy/support operator; real vendor regions and processor agreements; statutory tax/claims retention periods; backup expiry and deletion capability. A public policy is not proof that these tasks have been assigned or completed.
