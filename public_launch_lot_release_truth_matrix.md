## READER-FIRST CLASSICS

Reader-first classics are complete reader editions. Public copy should say reader-ready, not missing audio.

## SEO / LONG-FORM FLAGSHIP SHELF

Long-form classics build authority and search discovery. They should not be forced into first paid audiobook ads because QA and production cost risk are higher.

## Prohibited Public Claims

- “All Tier 1 audiobooks are live.”
- “Full audiobook library live.”
- “Word-level sync” for paragraph/stanza titles.
- “Listen now” for any non-approved title.
- Any stale audio URL or browser/system speech fallback presented as an audiobook.
