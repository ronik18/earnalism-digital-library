Earnalism is a premium digital reading room for Bengali and English classics: reader-first editions, beautiful covers, and natural audiobooks only when quality gates pass.

## Bengali Positioning

Bengali classics should feel native, not translated or secondary. Lead with Bengali titles, Bengali captions, and reader-first respect. Audio is a premium layer, not a promise until proven.

## English Classics Positioning

## Audiobook Positioning
