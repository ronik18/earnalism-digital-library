# Narrative performance, continuity and final scored review

Owner editorial direction, 1 November 2026. Apply to all future Earnalism audiobook generation and all new or revised listening editions. For unchanged retained editions, reuse applicable evidence, but a new material owner finding reopens the affected gate. These instructions grant no new synthesis, allocation, provider retry, purchase, external transfer or customer debit.

For the diagnostic decision table, TTS-text normalization, supported runtime controls, sample-based edit list, dramatic cue workflow and mastering checks, read [evidence-led voice engineering](voice-engineering.md). It turns these editorial requirements into a source-bound repair and prevention method.

## Understand the whole work before directing it

Read the complete authoritative book before choosing the final narrator or producing new narration. Understand each sentence and phrase in the context of its scene and the whole work: narrative viewpoint, speaker, relationships, subtext, irony, reveals, emotional progression and ending. A title, synopsis or genre label is insufficient. If full text is unavailable, mark comprehension incomplete and hold production; never invent context.

Keep a source-bound performance plan internally, identifying the edition/text hash, overall interpretation, narrator rationale, pronunciation decisions and scene/phrase directions. Preserve the exact manuscript and wording. Performance annotations are separate control data, not additions to the spoken text.

Choose a suitable qualified male or female voice through actual language, accent, range, storytelling and pronunciation evidence. Avoid gender stereotypes and automatic title-based casting. Keep an accepted identity unless a demonstrated defect requires a supported repair. Radio-drama quality means vivid, coherent storytelling; it does not authorize added dialogue, omitted narration, a new cast or an imitation of a real person. Multiple voices require an explicitly approved cast and stable character assignments.

## Direct syntax, meaning and emotion together

For each sentence/phrase, determine who is speaking, the intention, grammatical structure, emphasis, emotional state and the transition into and out of it. Plan breathing and pauses around meaningful syntactic boundaries. A comma is not a mandatory fixed silence; a line break or processing-unit boundary is not automatically a pause. Preserve grammatical attachments, negation, names and connected phrases. Differentiate a genuine question, rhetorical question, interruption, hesitation, exclamation and a sentence ending from context.

Direct pitch and range, seriousness, gravity, anger, grief, tone, vocal texture, emotional intensity, questioning inflection, fear, horror, pace and emphasis only where the text supports them. Keep the underlying narrator identity recognizable. Let context determine expressive changes; avoid a fixed emotional preset, mechanical punctuation reading, random pitch variation, monotony and exaggerated acting. Natural, human-like storytelling is the target, not a capability claim or a promised perfect outcome.

Bind the selected voice to provider, exact voice ID, model/revision, generation request and applicable rights/consent, then corroborate it against actual audio. The same display name can refer to another provider or a custom voice. A filename, folder label, roster preference or different narrator's licence cannot qualify it. An explicit owner selection such as Girija overrides a default provider preference, but it cannot relabel retained Roopa audio or authorize a Tanishaa fallback.

Map those directions only to the actual voice/model's supported instructions or controls. Do not invent numeric API attributes or assume a speaker label guarantees an emotion. If the selected model cannot reliably convey a required passage within the authorized attempt budget, record the limitation and hold or prepare an authorized alternative; do not loop or silently use fallback TTS.

## One recognizable narrator across every unit

Freeze voice identity, accent, model/tokenizer/runtime revisions, reference voice where supported, recording characteristics and the base direction. Carry scene and neighbouring-sentence context into each unit using supported context controls; distinguish unspoken context from the exact text to narrate so it cannot be duplicated. Split on natural linguistic boundaries where practical. Resetting a unit must not reset the narrator's identity or the scene's emotional state.

Compare Unit 01 against Unit 02 explicitly, then every adjacent pair in the assembled master. Listen across each boundary, including complete sentences on both sides, for changes in speaker identity, timbre, pitch baseline, age impression, accent, texture, recording level and emotional continuity. Pinned voice IDs, waveform similarity and loudness matching do not prove perceptual continuity. An unintended impression of two different narrators is a material blocking defect.

Establish a coherent pause hierarchy for clauses, sentences, paragraphs, scene changes and chapters, tailored to the work. Consistency means comparable pauses for comparable linguistic purposes, not equal-duration gaps everywhere. Measure and review the rendered pauses, especially at unit joins. Check for doubled generated-plus-inserted silence, clipped breaths, awkward hesitation and rushed sentence starts. Do not force all punctuation into one millisecond rule.

Retain original stems and healthy audio. Where authorized, repair only demonstrated defects, then recheck the repaired unit and both neighbours. A silence-only edit must preserve words, phonemes, breaths and intended dramatic timing; a new derivative requires new hashes and affected measured alignment/navigation. Do not disguise an identity mismatch with pitch shifting, time stretching, EQ or music. A no-synthesis restriction still binds: document any delivery defect that editing retained assets cannot safely resolve.

## Compose a content-led instrumental score

For every new or revised final listening edition, plan and blend an appropriately licensed instrumental background score. Default to a scored edition for owner review, rather than presenting a dry diagnostic stem as the final audiobook. Use silence deliberately within the score where the text needs intimacy, clarity or dramatic space. A wholly dry final edition requires an explicit owner exception; unavailable suitable licensed music leaves the scoring requirement unresolved, not waived.

Treat unit boundaries as processing boundaries, not musical exits. Account for the entire narration timeline: every unit and join needs an audible cue or an explicitly documented, phrase-bound intentional silence for review. Check Unit 01 to Unit 02 to Unit 03 and every later transition. A short track ending, sparse cue sheet or music-only introduction must not conceal unscored units; cue coverage and genuine listening to the delivered mix are separate requirements. Check the final encoded delivery after the opening, across late units and at every track/ducking transition. Nonzero music samples or a present stem do not prove the listener can hear the intended score. Diagnose missing cues, mixing and player delivery separately before changing narration.

Make a cue sheet tied to the actual scene/phrase meaning and measured final narration: track/source and licence evidence, permitted commercial synchronization/mixing/distribution and attribution, asset hash, cue entry/exit, emotional purpose, gain/ducking and fades. Select motifs and instrumentation for the work's arc; avoid a generic loop, premature spoilers, lyrics under speech, abrupt mood changes or a score that dictates emotions absent from the text. Do not purchase, generate music or export private material without the applicable authority.

Keep narration dominant. Use restrained levels, speech-aware ducking and smooth fades; check quiet speech, dialogue, emotional peaks and cue/segment boundaries. Validate the actual final mix for intelligibility, masking, loudness consistency, peaks/clipping, artifacts and clean joins against the delivery specification. Listen at normal and low volume with appropriate available playback setups; label any unperformed device check unverified. Music is not a repair for unnatural narration or irregular timing.

## Review and share the exact final listener experience

Separate direction/production, critical listening, repair and release verification as evidence claims. One complete review of the exact final scored mix can assess narration and music together; do not demand a second full dry listen or repeat an accepted whole-book review for an unrelated metadata change. Retain dry stems and use targeted diagnostics where a material issue requires them. Check naturalness, expressive appropriateness, grammatical phrasing, pronunciation, voice identity, every transition, timing and music balance. Apply the current applicable acceptance policy without inventing scores or converting qualitative findings into numerical ones.

Before listening, declare the applicable per-book policy and any owner-specified target, named dimensions, scale and calculation. If an owner asks for a mean of 9.8 for each title, calculate it only from that title's genuine named dimension scores; keep overall and confidence separate, no rounding up or pooling books. Preserve platform per-dimension/confidence/fatal/objective gates. High averages cannot cancel content errors, identity mismatch, robotic texture, choppy joins or obscured words. A sample score does not carry to the complete book, and no new target retroactively changes historical or Bengali policy. An ambiguous confidence response is not a missing set of listening scores.

Always share the complete final user-facing audiobook with its background score for owner review and evaluation before release. Use a private supported preview or local artifact, identify the exact title/edition, duration and mix version/hash, and retain the observations. A dry stem, selected samples, a cue sheet or a differently mixed file does not fulfil this requirement. Any requested owner evaluation remains pending until actually performed; an intention to listen is not acceptance. Preserve existing access and debit limits when providing or checking a preview.

Do not substitute ASR, amplitude measurements, successful generation or a reviewer's self-declared PASS for genuine perceptual evidence. Use neutral unit identifiers and actual time boundaries; do not label unreviewed narration as silence or prime a reviewer with presumed quality. Check claimed coverage and findings against the submitted asset and known facts. Unsupported, contradictory or incomplete review stays unverified, even if it declares high confidence.

Known owner-reported defects block the affected edition until repaired and genuinely rechecked; older acceptance cannot override them. A repair or new score creates a new candidate: retain prior evidence/history, requalify affected content fidelity, joins, applicable text timing, delivery and the scored mix, and share that exact candidate again. Publish only the tested, reviewed mix with no unresolved material defects and all existing rights, storage, runtime, accounting and release gates satisfied. Make no absolute human-indistinguishability or flawless-experience claims.


Read-along, narration-linked highlighting and text-linked audio navigation are permanently retired from supported current and legacy customer paths. Do not offer, advertise or re-enable them from stored flags. Ordinary Reader page/chapter navigation, text selection/manual annotations, and native audio chapter/seek controls remain. Reuse historical measured evidence only in its actual scope; never delete it or relabel it PASS/N/A. Record candidate-bound NOT_APPLICABLE for a verified nonconsumer without fabricating cues; caption/cue-consuming production remains strict where actually selected. This source policy grants no runtime retry, allocation or release authority.
