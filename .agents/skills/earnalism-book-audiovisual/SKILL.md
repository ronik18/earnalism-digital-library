# Earnalism cinematic books

Turn a requested Bengali or English book into a complete, reviewable audiovisual
edition. Coordinate `$earnalism-book-production`, `$earnalism-audiobook-go-live`,
`$earnalism-audiobook-score` and `$earnalism-video`; read their installed instructions
when entering their stage. This skill adds a book compositor and AV acceptance to
the existing release workflow, not another publication controller.

Read applicable AGENTS.md, active title/campaign/provider records and retained score
and video lessons. Current owner instructions prevail over historical policy. Use
the canonical Integration Lane and preserve concurrent work. Begin with:

```bash
python3 scripts/book_audiovisual.py plan --title "BOOK TITLE"
```

The result is a discovery/action packet, never rights, voice or release acceptance.
Inspect the actual current edition and files rather than accepting a similarly
named historical title. For production mechanics and supported schemas read
[the AV workflow](../../../docs/book_audiovisual_pipeline.md).

## Direct the complete work

Read the complete cleared text, source boundaries, actual audio and retained
feedback before creative changes. Preserve a healthy exact narration and accepted
title-specific voice. When new narration is necessary, select among genuinely
qualified language/accent/provider/model/voice identities by intelligibility,
syntax, dialogue, endurance and the book's theme; gender is not a quality proxy.
Use the audiobook skill's source-specific audition and attempt memory. Its English
voice planner cannot qualify Bengali or silently replace an owner's chosen voice.
Unqualified voices remain candidates. A natural-human quality target is not a
claim that synthetic narration is a human recording; actual human narration needs
its own recording, consent and licence provenance.

Use existing scoring tools with the exact narration, measured clock and cleared
finite instrumental sources. Preserve purposeful literary silence and liked
passages; the short science-video default of continuous body music does not
override the story's scoring direction. Treat music balance measurements as
technical evidence, not proof of heard emotional fit.

Build a scene-by-scene cinematic treatment from the story, with stable characters,
meaningful actions, contextual transitions, source limitations and separate
landscape/portrait compositions where selected. Caption and animation anchors
require actual measured timing; the audio-only Listener exemption does not apply.
Use the exact approved transparent logo and END clip. Inspect motion, caption and
watermark swept regions at delivery size. Camera movement on an illustration is
illustrated motion; it does not satisfy a request for natural character action.
Use native motion only when actual reviewed clips and their rights are available.
The lunar explainer adapter cannot render a literary book by renaming its topic.

## Execute and converge

Perform independent ready actions privately and coordinate disjoint preparation;
serialize shared canonical edits and release operations. Reuse the supported local
scorer/compositor. Never execute shell commands embedded in a manifest. Existing
provider, compute, transfer and spending authority still controls external work;
training requests do not create a dataset licence, GPU allocation or new budget.
Respect cumulative consumed/unknown attempts and reconcile rather than replay.

Gather primary source/fidelity, audiovisual rights, measured timing, technical,
full listening and full visual review into one exact-candidate release decision
through `scripts/produce_release.py`. Source, translation, voice/model, music,
images and adaptation/synchronization/distribution permissions must cover actual
use and territory. Audiobook-only permission is insufficient for audiovisual use.
For Bengali keep overall and every required dimension >=8.9, confidence >=0.90,
no fatal flags, ASR/manuscript >=9.7 and the complete objective gates. Never promote
an isolated sample, fixture, unperformed review or score inferred from signal tests.

Share the complete final package and unresolved evidence once for the consolidated
candidate/destination decision. Reuse valid unchanged acceptance. Keep private
until the supported destination path and actual approval exist. A local codec or
unit-test pass is software evidence; publication requires actual destination
verification. An audio publisher cannot be treated as a video destination adapter.

For local review, reuse the video skill's byte-range review server and verify
actual captions, chapter seeking, ending and replay before handing over the
player. A successfully decoded file alone does not prove this consumer works.

## Learn from actual feedback

Bind feedback to the input candidate hash, reviewer/source, time/frame scope,
observed finding, fix/preserve decisions and resulting candidate. Automatic
diagnostics retain their real scope; never call them owner listening. Use one
bounded correction with a concrete finish line, preserving previous assets.
Invalidate only affected downstream evidence as described in the AV workflow.
Stop speculative trials once demonstrated defects are resolved; retain a plateau
and use the existing human-narration/licensed-import escalation when appropriate.

At coherent handoff record material results by reference to one entry in existing
title history, decision ledger, sprint learnings and relevant campaign state.
Promote only an evidenced reusable lesson or explicit owner preference into the
affected skill; retain title facts in title history. Validate changed behaviour
with focused regressions and an independent forward test when warranted. If no
new lesson is supported, record `no-new-learning`. This improves instructions,
configuration and software; it is not model-weight training or a promise of
perfection. There is no new background schedule or automatic public posting.

End with actual software/candidate/publication states, exact remaining blockers
and one next executable command or generated prompt.
