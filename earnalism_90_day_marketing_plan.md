## Channel Strategy

### YouTube Shorts
- Audience: Bengali/English literature discovery, students, quote-driven readers.
- Formats: 15-30s quote reveals, “why this classic still hurts,” cover-to-reader walkthroughs, approved audio snippets only.
- Frequency: 4 Shorts/week.
- CTA: “Read the edition” or “Listen in Earnalism” only for approved audio.
- KPI: 3-second hold, average view duration, reader starts, listen starts.
- Split: organic first; boost only top retention clips.

### YouTube Long-Form
- Audience: literature learners and trust-builders.
- Formats: 6-10 minute author/title explainers, Bengali classic context, build-in-public quality gates.
- Frequency: 1/week.
- CTA: newsletter signup and title shelf.
- KPI: watch time, subscriber conversion, reader starts.

### Instagram Reels
- Audience: visual literary discovery, Bengali diaspora, Bookstagram.
- Formats: quote overlays, premium cover motion, “reader edition live,” approved audio snippets.
- Frequency: 4-5 Reels/week.
- CTA: Library link, save/share, read title.
- KPI: saves, shares, profile link clicks, reader starts.

### Instagram Carousels
- Audience: slower literary browsers.
- Formats: 5-slide title cards, author context, “read/listen truth” explainer.
- Frequency: 2/week.
- CTA: save this classic, browse shelf.
- KPI: saves, completion, shares.

### Facebook Pages/Groups
- Audience: Bengali community readers, education groups, classics readers.
- Formats: respectful excerpt cards, Bengali launch posts, no spam.
- Frequency: 3 posts/week plus selective group participation.
- CTA: read the free/public-domain edition.
- KPI: link clicks, comments, community approvals.

### WhatsApp Broadcast/Community
- Audience: high-trust Bengali reader circles.
- Formats: weekly “3 classics to read,” approved audio announcement, quote card.
- Frequency: 1-2/week.
- CTA: read/listen title.
- KPI: click-through, forwards, return visits.

### TikTok / BookTok-Style Videos
- Audience: fast literary discovery.
- Formats: native vertical 9:16, first-3-second hook, captions, creator/person-forward explainers.
- Frequency: 3-5/week.
- CTA: read/listen in profile.
- KPI: retention, shares, profile clicks.

### LinkedIn Build-in-Public
- Audience: founders, educators, publishing/AI quality observers.
- Formats: release-gate lessons, Bengali audiobook quality story, no hype.
- Frequency: 2/week.
- CTA: follow the launch, join newsletter.
- KPI: profile visits, newsletter signup, partner replies.

### Reddit / Quora
- Audience: authentic literature and Bengali communities.
- Formats: answer-first posts, reading lists, no direct promo unless relevant.
- Frequency: 2 high-quality contributions/week.
- CTA: soft profile/link only where allowed.
- KPI: accepted posts, referral visits, zero moderation issues.

### Google Search Ads
- Audience: high-intent searches for public-domain classics, Bengali classics, audiobook title searches.
- Formats: tightly themed responsive search ads, title landing pages, sitelinks by shelf.
- Frequency: small test after tracking is live.
- CTA: Read now / Listen only for approved audio.
- KPI: CTR, cost per reader start, cost per signup.
- Guardrail: use exact/phrase high-intent themes first; do not use broad spend until conversion data exists.

### SEO Landing Pages
- Audience: search-driven readers.
- Formats: title pages, author shelves, Bengali classics landing page, approved audiobook shelf.
- Frequency: 2-3 page improvements/week.
- KPI: indexed pages, impressions, book detail views, reader starts.

### Email Newsletter
- Audience: retained readers.
- Formats: weekly “quiet classics” digest; one Bengali + one English recommendation.
- Frequency: weekly.
- KPI: signup rate, open rate, click-to-reader-start.

## 90-Day Phases

1. Days 1-15: Build organic proof with reader-ready titles and approved audio only.
2. Days 16-30: Launch Founding Lot waitlist/newsletter and first paid search micro-test.
3. Days 31-60: Publish additional gate-approved audio candidates; retarget visitors; build author/genre shelves.
4. Days 61-90: Scale winning short-video hooks and launch a 5-title next wave only if release gates keep passing.

## Source Guidance Used

- Google Ads: use responsive search ads with strong/varied assets, specific CTAs, and landing-page match.
- YouTube: create audience-resonant videos and use analytics/community engagement rather than chasing algorithm hacks.
- TikTok: vertical 9:16, early hook, captions, creator-native style, test 3-5 creatives/ad group, refresh creative when fatigue appears.
